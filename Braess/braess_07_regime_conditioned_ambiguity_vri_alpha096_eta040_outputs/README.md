
Regime-conditioned ambiguity outputs:
- `braess_regime_scenario_library.csv`: regime probabilities, conditional probabilities, radii, and standardized support coordinates.
- `braess_regime_conditioned_summary.csv`: empirical mixture SP, fixed-frequency conditional DRO, joint-frequency regime DRO, and regime-informed benchmark.
- `braess_regime_informed_by_regime.csv`: separate regime-wise path shares, conditional MeanTest/CVaRTest/CETest, and objectives.
- `braess_regime_vri_decomposition.csv`: weighted informed objective and VRI decomposition.
- `braess_regime_path_shares.png`: comparison of ex ante and regime-informed path shares.
